Overview

Team Branding Profile
The Goal

Give team leaders a free, self-service way to create a team presence on realtor.com. No sales call, no paperwork. Just sign up and go live.

What is a "Lead Agent"?

A lead agent is the MLS-verified real estate agent who leads a team. They're the one who:

  • Creates the team profile (name, photo, bio, service areas)
  • Invites other agents to join the roster
  • Manages and edits the profile going forward

Think of them as the "team captain." Right now, only they can set up and manage the profile. Other agents on the team are invited and simply accept or decline.

Important: This is a Free Profile Only

This version only includes the free Branding Team Profile. No LEX ads, no paid features, no premium placement. It's a free profile, just like the individual agent profiles already in our directory today. Once published, the team becomes visible in realtor.com's public Agent Directory, the same way an individual agent's profile is.

Where This Lives: ProDashboard Trigger, RealProDashboard Build

Because we're starting with the agent, not an admin, the entry point (the action that kicks off profile creation) lives in ProDashboard, where agents already work today.

The actual experience is being built entirely in RealProDashboard, following our shell pattern. We're doing this intentionally so we don't take on design and engineering debt later. This means building it right the first time in the platform we're consolidating toward, rather than building it in the old system and having to redo it down the line.

Important details
  • MLS-verified teams only: Only agents with a verified MLS ID can create or be invited to join a Branding Team Profile, ensuring every listed team is authentically licensed.
  • Lead agent creates and manages: The lead agent builds the profile (name, photo, bio, areas served, years of experience, languages, specialties) and invites or removes roster members — all from the RealPro Dashboard, accessed via the new "Branding Team Profile" nav item in ProDash.
  • Roster-based aggregation: Reviews, listings, and price range are automatically rolled up from accepted roster members — the lead agent never maintains these manually.
  • Participating agents keep their identity: An accepted agent's individual profile continues to display standalone and is also surfaced within the team profile — it is never merged or hidden.
  • Publish and go live immediately: The team profile is eligible to appear in the consumer directory as soon as the lead agent publishes — no invited agent needs to accept first. Aggregated stats populate as the roster grows.

Expectations

Team Branding Profile
Walkthrough expectations

Designs shown today reflect what we plan to deliver for the alpha release of the Branding Team Profile creation and roster management flow.

We'll also walk through the consumer-facing side of this launch — how a consumer discovers, evaluates, and engages with a team through the new agent search directory:

  • Search & discover: How a consumer searches the new agent directory and filters results (by location, specialty, team size, etc.) to find the team that best fits their needs.
  • Evaluate: How they view a team's profile page — roster, agent bios, credentials, branding, and active listings — to assess fit.
  • Engage: How they initiate contact with that team directly from the profile (e.g., a lead form or message to the team).

Any adjustments to core requirements will need to go through our Change Request log to preserve the integrity of the plan already in place and being worked on by engineering.
